# Agent WhatsApp Guidelines

For every agent working the Omnichannel Inbox on Badar Trader CRM. Written
at Muhammad's request, 2026-09-02.

## The two numbers

- **+92 371 5773903** - the live production number. Real customer leads
  from the Meta ad campaign land here. This is where you actually work
  customer conversations.
- **+971 52 558 6541** - the chatbot testing number. Used to try out and
  train the AI chatbot before anything it says or does reaches a real
  customer on 3903.

Both numbers share the same underlying WhatsApp system, but they serve
different purposes. Do not confuse "testing the bot" with "talking to a
real customer" - they are separate numbers for exactly that reason.

## Text one of these numbers yourself, at least once every 24 hours

This is not optional and it is not busywork. Here is exactly why, in plain
terms.

WhatsApp enforces a rule called the 24-hour customer service window: a
business can only send a free-form message to a phone number if that phone
has messaged the business within the last 24 hours. If your number hasn't
texted in, WhatsApp silently blocks the CRM from reaching you - no error
on your end, just nothing arriving.

This is a **real, confirmed problem happening right now**, not a
theoretical one. Checked live on 2026-09-02: every single new-lead
notification the CRM has tried to send to Ehsan's and Hanzala's numbers has
failed delivery. The CRM successfully asked WhatsApp to send it, but the
phone never received it, almost certainly because of this exact 24-hour
rule.

**What to do about it:** send any message at all - even just "hi" - to
whichever number applies to you, at least once every 24 hours. That
resets your own 24-hour window and lets the CRM's notifications, ping
alerts, and any chatbot test messages actually reach your phone again.

If you have gone a day or more without texting either number and then
notice you aren't getting notified about new leads, this is almost
certainly why. Text the number, then check again.

## Staying connected for chatbot training

The same 24-hour rule applies to 6541. If you want to see how the chatbot
actually responds, test a change, or report something it got wrong, you
need to have texted 6541 recently yourself - otherwise its replies can't
reach you either, for the same reason as above.

Practical habit: whenever you're about to review or test the bot, send it
a message first if you haven't in the last day, then proceed.

## Working conversations, no matter who a lead is assigned to

A lead being assigned to a colleague is not a reason to be blocked from
seeing or acting on a conversation when it genuinely needs attention today.
If you hit a message saying you don't have access to a conversation and
you believe that's wrong for a real, current reason, flag it immediately
rather than leaving a customer waiting - don't assume it's intentional.

## Summary

1. Text 3903 or 6541 yourself at least once every 24 hours - whichever
   applies to what you're doing.
2. This is what keeps WhatsApp notifications and bot test messages able to
   reach your phone at all.
3. If notifications suddenly stop, this is the first thing to check.
4. Report chatbot behavior you want changed by flagging it directly so it
   can be logged and reviewed properly, not by trying to fix it yourself.
