- [ ] **THE REAL ONE: two different responders answer the same message, and they contradict each other.** Screenshot 3 and 4: she typed "Urdu mai baat karo" and then "Ya offer kya hai." Each time she got TWO replies - the AI's generic acknowledgement ("Apka sawaal mausool ho chuka ha.   **Why:** the scripted button flow and the AI responder both handle the same inbound message.   **This is a design decision, not a typo, and it needs Muhammad's call.** Options, roughly in order of effort: (1) when the customer types free text mid-flow, let the AI answer and do NOT re-send the button prompt on that turn - re-send it only on the next unmatched message; (2) let the AI answer and then append the buttons to the AI's own reply, so it is one message not two; (3) suppress the AI entirely while a lead is inside the scripted funnel, and let it answer only at stages with no pending question. **Recommendation: option 1** - it is the smallest change, it stops the double-reply, and it keeps the AI useful for exactly the questions the buttons cannot answer ("ya offer kya hai" being the perfect example).
